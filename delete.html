Delete your Sprout account

This page is for the Sprout app on Google Play (package com.devansh.sprout). You can ask us to delete your account and the data we hold about you.

How to ask

  1. Email works.devansh.bhardwaj@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Sprout account".
  2. Say which sign-in you use: the phone number or the Google email address on your Sprout account. Do not send passwords or one-time codes.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you. We delete the account within 30 days of your request and tell you when it is done.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in record (phone number or Google email address) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your name, access record and any profile settings, such as the product-update email choice, stored in Firebase.
  • Usage counts and device and app information sent to Firebase Analytics, if you had agreed to share them, where the analytics service allows deletion by account.

What stays, and where

  • Your transactions, categories, budgets, goals and other records are stored only on your phone. We never receive them, so we cannot delete them for you. Remove them by opening Account in Sprout and choosing "Delete my data", or by uninstalling the app.
  • Analytics data that Firebase has already combined into anonymous totals cannot be traced back to you and may remain.
  • We keep a short record of your deletion request (the date and the fact it was done, without your sign-in details) for up to 12 months.
