Sprout Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Sprout is a personal finance app for Android. It shows where your money goes. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your transactions stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your transactions, categories, budgets, goals, recurring payments and splits are stored on your phone only. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Bank messages (SMS): if you choose "Sync from SMS" and allow the SMS permission, Sprout reads bank transaction alerts in your inbox on the device, lists the transactions it finds, and you decide what to save. Messages from other senders are ignored. Message text is not sent off the phone.
  • Bank statements and files you import are read on the device and are not uploaded.
  • If you switch on optional "Auto-sync", Sprout reads new bank alerts each time you open it. It is off by default and you can switch it off in Account at any time.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details. If you sign in with a phone number, we receive that number. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google email address. This is handled by Firebase Authentication and is used only to manage your account.
  • Name (optional). You may enter a name, which can be prefilled from Google. It is used for greetings and reminders. It is stored on your phone and in your own record in Firebase.
  • Product updates by email (optional). Your email is used for product updates only if you switch on "Product updates by email". It is off by default and you can switch it off in Account.
  • Account record. A small record in Firebase that says whether your account has access to the app.
  • Usage counts (optional). Only if you agree on the in-app card, Sprout sends counts of which features are used (a short fixed name for each event, such as "statement imported"). These never include amounts, merchants, message text or other financial content. Along with them Firebase receives its standard device and app information: app instance ID, phone model, Android version, country and app version. Advertising ID collection is switched off.

How we use it

To sign you in, run the app, personalise greetings and reminders, send product updates you asked for, and understand which features are used so we can improve them. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. Sprout shows no ads.

Who processes it

We use Google's Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Analytics) as a service provider to run sign-in, store your account record and receive the optional usage counts. Data is encrypted in transit. Firebase's own privacy information applies to its handling of that data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Your choices

  • Turn off usage sharing in Account at any time. This also clears Firebase's analytics data on your phone.
  • Turn off Auto-sync, reminders and product-update emails in Account.
  • Revoke the SMS permission in your phone's settings. Sprout still works from statements and manual entry.
  • Delete your data: use "Delete my data" in Account, or email us at the address below and we will delete your account record.

Children

Sprout is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
